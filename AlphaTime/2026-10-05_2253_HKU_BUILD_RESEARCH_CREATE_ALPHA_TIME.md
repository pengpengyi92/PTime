## 1. Night field nodes

### HKU Public Piano
- Official location: 5/F, Chong Yuet Ming Cultural Centre.
- HKU's first public piano was launched in 2021.
- Created and hand-painted by HKU students.
- Official invitation: available for the HKU community to enjoy and play freely.
- Field observation: Yamaha-branded piano observed tonight.
- PMAP role: PREST / PCCT / music / spontaneous creation / social node.
- Important: "play freely" does not automatically mean unrestricted 24/7 public access. Re-check building access before treating it as a midnight practice room.

### Yuet Ming Auditorium / Chong Yuet Ming Cultural Centre
- Cultural Centre spans 3/F–5/F.
- Yuet Ming Auditorium is on 5/F and is used for lectures and cultural performances.
- The centre also includes Studio 303, rehearsal / exhibition / busking functions.
- PMAP role: arts + performance + student culture + recovery / creation layer.

### Meng Wah Complex
- Opened 1995.
- Twin towers: Wong Chue Meng Building + Wong Chuang Lai Wah Building.
- Historically housed Social Sciences, Business and other academic units.
- Today houses Faculty of Education, CEDARS, university medical/dental clinics and a rooftop farm.
- Field observation tonight: classrooms and offices remain integrated into the hillside circulation.
- PMAP role: student services + education + health + academic / administrative node.

### School of Future Media / Digital Media Lab
- School of Future Media was established in Oct 2025.
- It combines Journalism and Media Studies Centre, Documentary & AI Filmmaking, and Global Creative Industries.
- The School's historic home is Eliot Hall.
- Digital Media Lab is EHG01B, G/F, Eliot Hall.
- Therefore: do NOT currently label the observed construction as a verified "Future Media Building" unless the exact construction sign confirms it.
- Treat tonight's construction as FIELD-OBSERVED / NEEDS-DD.
- PCCT relevance: journalism + documentary + AI content generation + global creative industries.

## 3. Professor compensation

Current 2026 HKU professoriate recruitment advertisements generally do NOT publish a uniform numeric salary band.

Current official package language includes:
- highly competitive salary commensurate with experience
- annual / professional leave
- medical benefits
- contract-end gratuity + university retirement contribution totaling up to ~15% of basic salary
- housing benefits where applicable
- selected research-heavy appointments include tailored start-up packages

Conclusion:
Professor compensation is clearly competitive, but use current offer-level evidence rather than stale online salary tables.

## 4. Where the PhD "office" actually is

This is the most actionable finding tonight.

For Faculty of Engineering:
- Research Student Centre (RSC)
- Room G-01, Ground Floor, Haking Wong Building
- ~750 m²
- houses 140+ research postgraduate students
- mixes students across 5 departments based on interdisciplinary research interests
- 5 discussion rooms
- pantry / write-up / reading / locker infrastructure
- open Mon–Sun 09:00–24:00 except public / university holidays

This directly validates the imagined daily PhD operating loop:
home -> HKU -> RSC / lab / supervisor -> seminar -> paper / coding -> dinner / campus walk -> home.

Note:
Not every HKU PhD has one central universal office. Office / desk location depends on faculty, school, lab and supervisor. Engineering has this unusually explicit shared RPG infrastructure.
