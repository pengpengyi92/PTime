# GOD Note — P Benchmark Five / Real Estate Was Born from Hong Kong Field Observation

**Date:** 2026-10-04  
**Origin context:** Wan Chai → Central → Lan Kwai Fong / Mid-Levels field run.

## Origin
P Benchmark Five / JMatrix5 Real Estate emerged from observing Hong Kong as a real relocation system rather than an abstract housing market.

Wan Chai provided a key inspiration:
- international professionals / residents;
- Grade-A offices;
- finance / FinTech / corporate buildings;
- nightlife / restaurants;
- residential blocks;
- short commuting distances;
- very different rent / lifestyle combinations within a small area.

The insight was:
**housing selection must be linked directly to job, salary, office, commute, lifestyle and recovery.**

## Reusable sequence
**job / PhD / office**
→ **salary / funding**
→ **housing budget**
→ **commute radius**
→ **district**
→ **building**
→ **suite**
→ **landing-in**

## Why it belongs in PTime
A relocation framework is also a time framework.

Good housing reduces:
- commuting friction;
- search time;
- decision latency;
- recovery loss;
- unnecessary transitions.

Therefore Real Estate is part of AlphaTime infrastructure.

## P Global
The same system should transfer across:
- Hong Kong;
- Shenzhen;
- Singapore;
- London;
- future global offices / PhD locations.

## Principle
> A city visit compounds when it creates decision infrastructure that can be reused in the next city.
