# GOD Note — T → T+1 Markov Time Protocol

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-05 17:24 HKT

## Core idea
Treat life as a state-transition process.

At time **T**, the objective is not merely to produce immediate output.  
The objective is to choose actions that improve the quality and option set of **T+1**.

In simplified form:

**State(T) + Action(T) + Environment(T) + RiskControl(T) → Distribution of State(T+1)**

This is the PPIME / PTime Markov-chain intuition.

## Practical implications
Every T should try to increase one or more of:
- Output
- Connection
- Observation
- Recovery
- Capital
- Skill
- Optionality

while reducing:
- avoidable distraction;
- low-value conflict;
- irreversible downside;
- unnecessary context switching;
- energy depletion that damages the next state.

## Hong Kong example
A good sequence is:

**HK fieldwork → identify Wan Chai as T0 base → map Admiralty / Central → build relationships → secure role / RA / PhD → rent and land → compound over years.**

Each step makes the next state easier and higher-quality.

## Time principle
**Time is precious.**
Do not maximize busyness.  
Maximize the expected quality of the next state.

A successful T is one that creates a stronger T+1.
