## 2. Density × Flow -> more observable opportunity

Working analogy:

```text
high density
× high flow
× repeated interaction
× low switching / movement friction
→ more observable opportunities
```

This resembles a market-liquidity intuition: opportunity discovery is easier where information, people, capital, institutions and activity circulate frequently.

Boundary:

> **Urban / career "liquidity" is an analogy, not literal financial arbitrage.**
> More density does not guarantee better outcomes; conversion still requires capability, judgment, trust and execution.

## 6. "Global pay" = ecosystem participation, not geography alone

A Hong Kong role at a global institution may expose the worker to a different compensation market than a domestic institution, but pay is determined by:
- employer;
- role;
- seniority;
- performance;
- labor market;
- business economics;
- location;
- tax / benefits;
- negotiation and mobility.

So the useful concept is:

```text

→ become benchmarked against a different economic system
→ potential access to globally competitive compensation
```

not:

```text
Hong Kong address = guaranteed global pay
```
