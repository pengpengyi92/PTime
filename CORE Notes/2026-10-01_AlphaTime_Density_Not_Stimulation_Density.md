# AlphaTime Density > Stimulation Density

**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**System:** PTime / ETM-AAO

## Core distinction

The objective is not to eliminate pleasure or leisure.

The objective is to stop confusing **stimulation density** with **life density**.

A high-value hour should ideally leave one or more durable returns:

- People
- Knowledge
- Health
- Capital
- Artifact
- Opportunity
- Intentional Recovery

## AlphaTime test

Before spending an hour, ask:

> **When this hour ends, what remains?**

Valid outcomes include:
- a strong conversation;
- a new friend / partner / mentor;
- a piece of code;
- a research insight;
- an application or follow-up;
- a workout;
- a restorative meal;
- quality sleep;
- a durable relationship;
- a concrete next opportunity.

## Low-return loop

Pornography, repeated masturbation, purposeless nightlife, random urban wandering, or any other immediate-reward behavior are not treated as moral failures.

They become a PTime problem when they:
- displace intended work;
- fragment attention;
- reduce energy;
- create next-day aftershock;
- produce no durable return;
- become the default response to unstructured time.

## Operating rule

> **Do not optimize for stimulation. Optimize for AlphaTime density.**

A good day can include pleasure and rest, but they should support rather than consume the next high-quality block.

## Futian Base implication

The Shenzhen / Futian base is valuable because it makes high-quality transitions easy:

**work → meal → meeting → walk → research → home → recovery**

with low friction.

That is exactly what PTime should exploit.
