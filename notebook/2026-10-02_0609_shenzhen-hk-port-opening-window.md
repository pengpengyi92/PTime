## Why the 06:00 Huanggang queue exists

At 06:00, Huanggang is already operating while the other three major passenger ports are still closed.

Therefore early passengers naturally concentrate at Huanggang before 06:30.

Possible passenger types include:
- early commuters
- shift workers
- business travellers
- airport / leisure travellers
- other cross-border passengers

## Routing heuristic

00:00–06:30:
