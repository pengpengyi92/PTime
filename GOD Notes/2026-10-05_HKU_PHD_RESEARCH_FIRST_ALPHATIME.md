# 22:56 HKT — HKU PhD Research-First AlphaTime

Timestamp: 2026-10-05 22:56 HKT

## Principle

Campus energy is valuable, but PhD AlphaTime should stay research-first.

```text
research
-> paper
-> feedback
-> stronger paper
-> internship / collaboration
-> thesis
-> graduation
-> more research / impact
```

Culture / music / campus events are optional recovery infrastructure.

They are useful when they improve energy and T+1, but they do not replace:
- research quality;
- paper pipeline;
- thesis progress;
- supervisor alignment;
- Funding / Direction / Autonomy.

## State transition

Current visit should convert into:
- supervisor shortlist;
- research-fit note;
- application;
- meeting request;
- paper / project plan.

## Centennial Campus note

Centennial Campus is an active HKU campus expansion opened in 2012, connected to Main Campus through University Street.

It is part of the current operating campus, not a retired historic site.
