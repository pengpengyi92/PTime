# 2026-09-23 — PTime Before-Sleep Queue

Validated a strong PTime use case:

**Before Sleep → Think Through Tomorrow Queue → Next-Day Execution and Close**

Night should be for short planning, not extended deep work.

A useful Tomorrow Queue contains:
- who / what
- why
- next action
- best time
- channel
- deadline
- close condition

Example next-day targets:
- Tencent FIT due diligence / informal meeting
- HKU DS student due diligence / informal meeting

PTime should make tomorrow easier by removing morning uncertainty.

Core rule:
**Tonight decides the queue. Tomorrow executes and closes it.**
