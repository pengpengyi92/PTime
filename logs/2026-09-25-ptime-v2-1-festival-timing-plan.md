# 2026-09-25 — Festival Timing / General Greeting Upgrade

Decision:
PTime should gain a Greeting / Festival Timing layer.

Motivation:
Recurring festivals create natural, low-friction relationship touchpoints that can support later conversation, DD, collaboration, recruiting and follow-up.

Planned V2.1 scope:
- Chinese / Greater China festival calendar;
- Global festival calendar;
- General / Professional / Reconnect greeting templates;
- festival-aware contact timing;
- reply-triggered follow-up suggestions;
- human approval only;
- no autonomous sending.

Version note:
PTime is already V2.0.0 as of 2026-09-21. This feature is therefore planned as V2.1 rather than overwriting V2.0 history.
