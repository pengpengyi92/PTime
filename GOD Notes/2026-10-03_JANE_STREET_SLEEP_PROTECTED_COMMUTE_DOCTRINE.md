# Jane Street Workday Rhythm — Sleep-Protected Commute Doctrine

Date: 2026-10-03

## Core principle

A future Jane Street workday should be designed backwards from reliable arrival and adequate sleep.

The objective is not merely "wake up early".
The objective is:

**sleep protection -> early buffer -> reliable commute -> calm arrival -> full cognitive performance**

## Two viable living modes

### Mode A — Futian base + Hong Kong commute

This is viable when:
- the prior evening ends early
- border / rail variability is buffered
- departure time is early enough to reach Central before the workday
- the commute itself remains sustainable over repeated weekdays

Operational rule:
- no default late-night Hong Kong wandering before a workday
- return to Shenzhen early
- sleep approximately 8 hours
- leave enough margin for border and rail variance

### Mode B — Hong Kong residence

This reduces cross-border variance but still requires:
- housing with low-friction access to Central
- a consistent early-sleep / early-rise rhythm
- enough morning buffer to arrive before the desk starts

Kowloon Station / Hong Kong Island residential nodes can be evaluated partly through this lens.

## Example timing logic

If a desk day starts around 08:30 and the target is to be physically ready around 08:00:
- wake time may need to be around 07:00-07:15 when living close in Hong Kong
- an 8-hour sleep target implies roughly 23:00 bedtime
- Futian commuting requires a materially earlier departure and therefore usually an earlier sleep schedule

These are planning examples, not confirmed Jane Street official working hours.

## Career-performance interpretation

For PBCT-intensive work:
- coding
- reasoning
- market judgment
- debugging
- communication
- rapid learning

sleep is part of technical performance, not merely lifestyle.

For PACT and PCCT:
- conversational quality
- emotional regulation
- presence
- clarity
- collaboration

sleep also protects social and communication quality.

## PTime rule

**Never trade tomorrow's high-value cognitive window for low-value late-night drift.**

Late-night exploration can be worthwhile on holidays / deliberate field-DD days, but not as the default operating mode once a demanding work schedule begins.
