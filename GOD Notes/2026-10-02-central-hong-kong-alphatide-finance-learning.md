## Definition

Central is now classified as an **AlphaTide environment**:

> A place where the density of institutions, people, architecture, transport and business models materially raises the probability of high-value observations and actions per unit time.

- banking;
- insurance;
- custody / fund services;
- market making;
- hedge funds;
- prop trading;
- cross-border finance;
- hospitality;
- transport;
- career / recruiting targets.

## PTime rule

Central should **not** become aimless wandering.

Use it when at least one objective exists:
- company DD;
- interview;
- application preparation;
- coffee chat;
- hosting;
- commute test;
- finance-system learning;
- recovery attached to a real task.

## Star Ferry as time design

Pier 7 / Star Ferry can transform transport into:
- recovery;
- friend hosting;
- harbour orientation;
- low-cost experience.

Use when it is attached to an actual Central / Kowloon route, not as an unplanned detour.

## Permanent principle

> **Design the destination first, then enter the time block.**
