# Zhongshan Recovery + Global Informal Talk Node

Date: 2026-09-26

## Core Principle

Zhongshan is not an offline location. It is a **Recovery Base + Remote Informal Talk Base**.

When food, housing, family support, walking, rest and daily logistics are well covered, attention can shift toward work that does not depend on a Shenzhen office or physical presence:

- informal meeting / informal talk
- remote coffee chat
- recruiter call
- PhD student due diligence
- researcher / quant / FX talk
- follow-up and relationship maintenance
- global networking and opportunity discovery

## Same-Timezone Location-Independent Execution

Zhongshan, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Shanghai, Beijing, Hangzhou and the rest of mainland China share UTC+8.

Therefore the PTime communication layer should abstract away the specific mainland-China city:

**China Base Time (UTC+8) -> Global Communication Windows**

Moving between Shenzhen and Zhongshan should not require rebuilding the communication cadence, calendar logic or global outreach infrastructure.

## UTC+8 Talk Windows

- Morning / noon: China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan, Korea, Australia.
- Afternoon / early evening: Europe begins coming online.
- Evening: London / UK becomes especially comfortable for informal talks.
- Later evening: US East Coast begins to overlap; Boston and New York become reachable, followed by Chicago.

## City-Specific Information, Shared Time Layer

The timezone layer is shared, while the local information layer differs.

### Zhongshan
- recovery and family base
- strong food / housing / daily-life support
- walking, relaxing, thinking
- suitable for long-form informal talks
- can invite connections to visit, relax, walk and talk

### Shenzhen
- dense office / company / institution nodes
- physical networking and office visits
- interviews and in-person meetings
- also supports parks, walking and informal talks

The PTime layer is identical; PMap / local context determines what each city is best used for.

## System Linkage

PMap -> PTime -> PGlobal -> PLinkedIn -> PConnection -> Informal Meeting / DD / Relationship

- PMap: where I am and what local affordances exist.
- PTime: who in the world is currently in a good talk window.
- PGlobal: which geography / market / community to activate.
- PLinkedIn: discover and reach the right people.
- PConnection: maintain relationship state and follow-up history.
- Informal talk: convert remote availability into DD, knowledge and opportunity.

## Current Example

A high-value example is a Boston FX conversation with Xian Ming: use the late-evening UTC+8 window for a focused informal talk on FX research, team structure, data, strategy, execution and career path.

## Design Principle

**Recover locally, stay globally connected.**
