# GOD Note — Festival Timing as Relationship Infrastructure

Date: 2026-09-25

## Principle

PTime should manage not only timezone windows, but also naturally recurring relationship windows.

Festivals and holidays create low-friction moments for:
- relationship maintenance;
- reconnecting dormant contacts;
- warm follow-up;
- global networking;
- career / collaboration conversations after the initial greeting.

This creates a new timing dimension:

Clock Time
+ Timezone
+ Relationship State
+ Festival / Calendar Event
+ Channel
-> Communication Opportunity.

## General Greeting

General Greeting is the reusable relationship-maintenance counterpart to Self Introduction.

Self Introduction:
new relationship -> establish identity and context.

General Greeting:
existing relationship -> maintain warmth / reopen conversation.

The greeting itself should remain lightweight. Opportunity, DD, collaboration or referral discussion follows only after a natural reply or later follow-up.

## Two calendar views

### Chinese / Greater China Calendar
- Mid-Autumn Festival
- National Day
- New Year
- Spring Festival
- Lantern Festival
- Dragon Boat Festival

### Global Calendar
- New Year
- Christmas
- Thanksgiving
- Easter
- other region-specific official/public holidays when contextually appropriate.

## Long-run PTime role

PTime becomes:
WHEN by timezone
+ WHEN by follow-up cadence
+ WHEN by festival / relationship timing.

The goal is not mass messaging. The goal is timely, context-aware relationship maintenance.
