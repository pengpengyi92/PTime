# 2026-09-22 — PTime General Communication Timing & Scheduling Layer

## Scope Update

PTime should not be limited to overseas or global communication.

Its original motivation came from global time-zone coordination, but the general system is broader.

PTime is a **general communication timing and scheduling layer**.

## Core Function

PTime answers:

- who is contactable now;
- when is the best time to reach out;
- when should a meeting be scheduled;
- what communication channel fits the time window;
- when should a follow-up happen;
- how should domestic and global communication windows be coordinated.

## Coverage

PTime can support:

- domestic China communication;
- Shenzhen / Hong Kong meetings;
- WeChat outreach;
- phone calls;
- coffee chats;
- interviews;
- follow-ups;
- calendar scheduling;
- London / Europe windows;
- New York / Boston / Connecticut windows;
- Singapore / Asia windows;
- global multi-time-zone coordination.

## General Model

**People × Time × Channel × Opportunity**

The system should optimize communication timing, not merely convert time zones.

## Examples

Domestic:
- schedule a Shenzhen coffee chat;
- identify a lunch-break call window;
- arrange a Tencent informal meeting;
- plan same-day WeChat follow-up.

Global:
- switch from China work hours to London work hours;
- later switch to US East Coast availability;
- coordinate asynchronous follow-up across regions.

## Principle

Global communication is a major use case, not the boundary of PTime.

PTime's long-term role is:

> **General Communication Timing + Scheduling Infrastructure**

for both domestic and international relationship, recruiting, research, and business workflows.
