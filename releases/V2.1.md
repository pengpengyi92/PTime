# PTime V2.1 — Festival Timing & General Greeting

Status: CLOSED / PUBLISHED (documented environment limits retained)
Package version: 2.1.0
Plan date: 2026-09-25

## Theme

Festival-aware relationship timing.

## Why this release

PTime V2.0 determines communication windows using timezone, region, relationship, channel and context.

V2.1 adds a second timing layer:

Festival / Calendar Event
-> Natural Relationship Touchpoint
-> Greeting
-> Optional Follow-up.

This allows PTime to support recurring relationship maintenance without becoming a CRM or autonomous messaging system.

## Delivered scope

- Chinese / Greater China festival calendar
- Global festival calendar
- General Greeting templates
- Professional templates
- Reconnect templates
- festival-aware touchpoint ranking
- duplicate greeting suppression
- recent-contact suppression

- human approval for every action

## Relationship principle

Self Introduction establishes a relationship.

General Greeting maintains or reactivates a relationship.

Festival Timing provides the WHEN.

PTime should recommend timing; the human decides whether and what to send.
