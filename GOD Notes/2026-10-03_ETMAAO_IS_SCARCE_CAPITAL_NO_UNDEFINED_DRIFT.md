# ETMAAO Is Scarce Capital — No Undefined Drift

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**System:** PTime  
**Status:** Core GOD Note

## Principle

ETMAAO is scarce personal capital.

High-quality Energy, Time, Mind, Attention, Action and Opportunity windows should not be consumed by undefined drift.

The problem is not walking, eating, resting, exploring or observing. Those can all be high-value when deliberately assigned a mode.

The problem is continuing without a defined purpose and allowing the environment to consume attention by default.

## PTime Classification

Every active block should map to one of:

- **AlphaTime** — research, coding, applications, DD, building, trading, paper work, production.
- **PACT Time** — conversations, people graph, hosting, relationship development, institutional observation.
- **PBCT Time** — technical research, systems, coding, data, quant, experiments, artifacts.
- **Recovery Time** — sleep, meal, walking, sport, music, sea view, intentional decompression.
- **Transition Time** — commuting, border crossing, waiting; pair with rest, thinking, reading or review when useful.
- **Undefined Drift** — no clear Alpha, PACT, PBCT, Recovery or Transition objective.

Default rule:

> If the next 30 minutes cannot be classified, ETMAAO may be leaking.

## Harbour City Case

The prior Victoria Harbour / Harbour City wandering is retained as an ETMAAO case.

Walking itself was not the failure.

The failure mode was allowing part of the walk to become unbounded and undefined rather than:
- intentional Recovery,
- PMap field observation,
- PACT environment sensing,
- or a defined destination-driven block.

## Exit Rule

When Undefined Drift appears:

1. Name the current mode.
2. Assign a clear purpose and time box.
3. If no purpose exists, leave.
4. Route toward the next ALPHATIME node or return to recovery/base.

## Operating Doctrine

> PTime allocates ETMAAO.  
> Environment must not allocate ETMAAO by accident.

Every trip should protect the right to stop, exit, reroute and recover.
