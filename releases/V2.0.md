# PTime V2.0

## Theme
Global Communication Timing Layer.

## Major Upgrade
PTime connects temporal context with people, geography, relationship, channel and opportunities.

WHO + WHERE + WHEN + HOW -> ACTION

## New Integrations
P Email, P LinkedIn, P Connection, P Global and P Kago: normalized local YAML/JSON
snapshots and mock fixtures only. P Kago has no assumed native implementation.
No accounts are authenticated and no real source is imported automatically.

## Founding Scenario
At 2026-09-21 22:15 in Shenzhen, London is 15:15, New York/Boston 10:15, and San Francisco
07:15. Local professional activity can shift attention to another region, subject to
the user's own rest and bandwidth. These offsets are date-dependent, not constants.

## Objective
Use global time intelligently for professional communication, recruiting, interviews,
follow-ups, relationship maintenance and opportunity execution.

## Delivered Scope
- `now` and `contacts` retained.
- `talk`: best channel per unqueued contact, plus explicit queued actions.
- `email`: timing decisions for existing email queue records only.
- Eight channel rules, four contexts, personal windows, due/not-before fields.
- SEND_NOW / WAIT / REVIEW / BLOCKED / SKIP with explanations and dated next windows.
- Same-day suppression, no re-sending terminal actions, explicit late/scheduled exceptions.
- Source labels and immutable validated input snapshots.
